Privacy policy

Glass uses Google's Smart Device Management API only to list the owner's Nest cameras and to show their live video on the owner's own mirror at home, and only while someone has opened the camera view.

Glass does not record, store or upload camera video or snapshots, does not use camera events, and does not collect personal data. The sign-in keys stay on the mirror's own computer. Nothing is shared with anyone or used for advertising.

The owner can remove Glass's access at any time from Google's Partner Connections page (nestservices.google.com/partnerconnections).
